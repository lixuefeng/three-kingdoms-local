THE HOUSE RULES

Friends at the table. Rivals in the game.

Classic identity mode · 108 standard/EX cards, or 160 with Maneuver · 25 available generals

Your role. Your objective.

Lord & Loyalists: Eliminate every Rebel and the Spy.
Rebels: Eliminate the Lord, unless the Spy wins as the sole survivor.
Spy: Be the last survivor, eliminating the Lord last.
Only the Lord's role is public at the start. Other roles are revealed upon elimination.

Turns & responses

Preparation → Judgment → Draw → Play → Discard → End. Normally, draw 2 cards, use up to 1 Attack during your Play Phase, then discard down to your current HP. Skills and equipment can change these rules.

Use Peach to rescue a dying character. Negation cancels a Strategy's effect on one character; another Negation can restore it.

Seats & timers

Your general and skills share the bottom hand dock; opponents appear around the outer edges in seat order. The Lord keeps a red Lord badge, and starts with one extra current and maximum health in every room size (a house rule at four players). Seat numbers follow join order. Play moves counterclockwise through living seats; the Lord starts. Gold marks the active turn, teal marks the player making a decision. Each decision has its own timer, which resets for the next decision. Play decisions use the room’s selected time limit.

Playing at the table

Select cards and skills in your bottom hand dock, then click a highlighted player to choose a target. Selected cards rise above the hand. Select a card, select its targets in order, then click Confirm above your hand; Cancel is beside it. Only the info icon opens a general’s portrait and skills. Equipment is on the right of each player; click it directly when stealing or discarding, or use the large card chooser. Known roles use red for Lord, yellow for Loyalist, green for Rebel and purple for Spy; other players’ hidden roles stay neutral. Decision text on the table names the attacker when you need to respond. Health is shown as hearts. Pass-only responses happen automatically. Stargazing uses private top and bottom rows: drag cards, or use the move buttons, then confirm once.

Response shortcuts & testing

Pass for this spell skips further Negation prompts for the current spell, including its other targets and counter-Negations; the next spell asks again; Dodge and other responses still ask normally. For Bumper Harvest, click a revealed card at the center of the table. Enable Testing mode in the lobby to assign generals; the host’s Test draw button can give the current human player a selected card from the draw pile during their Play Phase. Enable Maneuver too to test expansion cards.

Game cards · WTK names

With Maneuver enabled: Flame Tactic is the fire spell; Fire Attack is the fire-damage Attack card. Iron Chains spreads elemental damage between chained characters. Recast Iron Chains to discard it and draw 1.

Meet the generals

This edition includes all 25 original standard generals and practice bots. The optional Maneuver pack adds game cards. Selected expansion generals adds 19 classic generals (44 total), independently of the card pack; Cao Pi, Jia Xu and Kingdom Wars are not included. Rooms and matches are kept in server memory and disappear after a server restart.

Portrait artwork and English names are referenced from the WTK general reference and WTK game card reference. Descriptions explain the classic rules implemented in this edition; newer card versions may differ.
